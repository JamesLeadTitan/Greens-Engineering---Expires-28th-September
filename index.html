About Us

Herefordshire Steel, Made On The Haywood Estate

We are Greens Engineering, a welding and fabrication workshop on the Haywood Industrial Estate in Wellington, just north of Hereford. We make steelwork for local builders, farms and homeowners, and we take the van out when the job cannot come to us.

We quote what the job actually needs. If a repair will do, we will tell you that instead of selling you a new one.
